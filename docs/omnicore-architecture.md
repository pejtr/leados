# OMNICORE Architecture

## CMS

Content management system for managing website content, articles, and pages.

## SEO Engine

Search engine optimization tools including keyword analysis, meta tag generation, and content scoring.

## Affiliate Tracking

Affiliate link management, commission tracking, and reporting.

## Marketplace / Catalog Engine

Product catalog management, pricing, inventory, and marketplace integrations.

## AI Assistants

AI‑powered chatbots and virtual assistants for lead qualification, customer support, and content generation.

## Leadgen

Lead generation tools including forms, landing pages, scoring, and CRM integration.

## Analytics

Business intelligence dashboards, reporting, and data visualization.

## Automation

Workflow automation, scheduled tasks, and event‑driven actions.

## Partner Integrations

Integration with third‑party services: Google Ads, Google Sheets, ClickUp, Slack, webhooks, ARES, etc.

## Future Telegram / Agent Bridge

Planned integration with Telegram for agent orchestration and communication.
