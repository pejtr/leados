# Revenue Leakage Audit Offer

## Verdict

Revenue Leakage Audit is the first sellable OMNICORE / OPTIMATEO product.

## Target Clients

Priority verticals:

1. Local premium services
2. Clinics
3. Real estate agents
4. B2B service companies

## Problem

The client has traffic, leads or campaigns, but does not know:

- where leads actually come from
- where conversion is lost
- which channels produce revenue
- which follow-up steps fail
- what to fix first

## Offer

A focused audit that identifies the top 3 revenue leaks and gives the client a prioritized action plan.

## Deliverables

- Funnel leakage map
- Source and attribution review
- Conversion bottleneck list
- Top 3 business-impact priorities
- Implementation roadmap
- Optional monthly monitoring plan

## Pricing

- Audit: 25k-75k CZK
- Implementation: 75k-250k CZK
- Monitoring: 10k-50k CZK/month

## Success Criteria

- Client understands where money is leaking
- Client receives top 3 priorities
- Each recommendation has clear business impact
- Audit can be sold before full ONYX OS implementation

## Out of Scope

- Guaranteed revenue growth without implementation
- Full CRM replacement
- Full marketing automation in MVP
